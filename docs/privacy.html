Privacy Policy

Who uses this app. Clip Autopilot is operated by and for a single TikTok creator (the owner of this repository) to publish their own content to their own account.

Data accessed. With the owner's authorization, the app uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API to read basic profile information (display name, avatar, privacy options) and to upload and publish videos. Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub settings and are used only to publish the owner's scheduled posts.

Data shared. No TikTok data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics. Nothing is collected from viewers or other TikTok users.

Retention and deletion. Rendered clips are deleted from storage after they are published. The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions, and can delete the stored tokens from the repository settings.

Contact. Open an issue on this repository.
